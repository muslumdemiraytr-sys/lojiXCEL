Calculate total freight for shipments from Istanbul to Ankara that were delivered on time in October.
Use this formula:
=SUMIFS(F:F, B:B, "Istanbul", C:C, "Ankara", H:H, ">="&DATE(2026,10,1), H:H, "<"&DATE(2026,11,1), J:J, "On time")
Explanation: Sums the freight amounts in column F for rows where the origin city (B) is Istanbul, the destination city (C) is Ankara, the delivery date (H) is on or after October 1, 2026 and before November 1, 2026 (i.e. within October), and the delivery status (J) is “On time”. The dates in column H must be real date values, not text. If you don’t have column J, you can build it by comparing the delivery date (H) with the planned date (G): =IF(H2="","",IF(INT(H2)<=G2,"On time","Late")). INT drops the time portion of H so a same-day delivery isn’t counted as late, and rows with an empty H (not yet delivered) are left blank. Turkish Excel uses localized function names (ÇOKETOPLA, TARİH) and semicolons as separators; LojiXcel generates those versions too.

J1FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Write to us.

What is LojiXcel?

An AI-powered Excel assistant for logistics and supply-chain teams. Upload your spreadsheet, ask questions in plain language, and get formulas, pivot tables, charts and reports.

Which file formats are supported?

.xlsx, .xls, .csv and Google Sheets. Exports from ERP, WMS and TMS systems can be used directly.

Does it work with exports from ERPs such as SAP, Logo or Netsis?

Yes. Just upload the Excel/CSV report from your system; column names and formats are recognized automatically.

Does it support localized Excel (e.g. Turkish)?

Yes. It writes formulas with English function names (SUMIFS, VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP) and with localized names, e.g. in Turkish Excel SUMIFS = ÇOKETOPLA, VLOOKUP = DÜŞEYARA and XLOOKUP = ÇAPRAZARA, using the argument separator your regional settings expect (comma or semicolon).

Which logistics KPIs can it calculate?

OTIF, on-time delivery (OTD), inventory turnover, days of inventory, warehouse utilization, picking productivity, cost per km/ton, order cycle time and more.

Can it create presentations (PowerPoint)?

Yes. It automatically builds your weekly operations report or customer QBR deck from your data.

Do I need a credit card?

No. The free plan does not require a credit card.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. You can cancel your subscription in one click from your account settings and keep using it until the end of the billing period.

Do I need Excel installed on my computer?

No. Everything runs in the browser; download the results as .xlsx and open them in any program you like.

Is my data secure?

Files are encrypted, never used for model training and can be deleted whenever you want. Data is processed in line with KVKK and GDPR.
